Tell us about the job.

Where earned profit leaks

Small gaps between teams become schedule, capacity, margin and cash problems.

01

Estimating assumptions get lost in the handoff.

Scope, exclusions, labor assumptions and procurement risks lose context as the job moves into execution.

02

Project teams operate in reaction mode.

PMs chase approvals, resolve shop and field questions, manage changes and update customers without a consistent operating rhythm.

03

Engineering and approvals dictate the schedule.

Incomplete inputs, slow decisions and shifting details compress purchasing, fabrication and installation windows.

04

Production absorbs upstream instability.

Priorities shift, information arrives late and work is released before every dependency is ready.

05

Teams and systems hold different versions of the truth.

Information is scattered across departments, spreadsheets, email and specialized software, forcing people to re-enter, reconcile and chase it.

06

Changes and billing fall behind the work.

Notice, pricing, authorization, progress billing, punch and closeout trail the value already delivered.
